Yidhan · design review · September 2026

Beautiful today. World-class next.

Ten pictures, few words. Everything here was seen in the real app, not guessed from the code. The full write-up is in the repo.

1 · Keep this

The look is yours alone.

No other notes app feels like this. And the privacy is real: notes are locked on your device before they leave it.

2 · The writing surface

Writing has small snags.

This is one test note. Every snag below happened in it.

1 2 3 4 5
  • 1I pressed Enter in the title. Everything after it went into the title too.
  • 2Pasted markdown stays as symbols instead of becoming a heading and a list.
  • 3Links work by accident. There is no link button, and clicking one leaves the app.
  • 4Pressing Esc to close this menu closes the whole note.
  • 5No pictures. No word count. No find in note.

Fix: one week of small changes. Then add pictures, locked like everything else.

3 · The library

Works at 40 notes. Not at 2,000.

The library has one idea about order: what you touched last.

1 2 3 4
  • 1Search re-reads every note on every key you press.
  • 2One pinned note, one big empty band.
  • 3Only one order: last edited. Editing an old note pulls it to the top.
  • 4No list view. No note has a link you can copy or come back to.

Fix: give every note a web address, add a list view and a quick switcher, make search fast and smart.

4 · On a phone

The first minute on a phone.

Two things a visitor sees before writing a word.

Fix: a one-line hint instead of a pop-up. Give the ribbon its own room. Then build the iPhone app with Face ID unlock.

5 · Trust

Forget your passphrase, lose everything.

Today the passphrase is the key. It cannot be changed, and there is no spare.

Fix: a spare key you keep on paper. Yidhan still cannot read your notes. You can no longer lose them.

6 · Sync

Three things that don't "just work" yet.

The sync engine is strong. Three gaps undo the feeling.

  • 1Tags added on one device don't show up on another until you sign in again.
  • 2When two devices disagree, the choice screen shows two blank cards. You pick blind.
  • 3Markdown export loses highlights and alignment. The full backup file can't be imported back.

Fix: all three are small. Do them first.

7 · Speed

A megabyte before the first word.

This is what the browser downloads to show the front page.

1,075 KBof code, for one headline and a button
App code788 KB
Database client189 KB
Styles79 KB
Error reporting19 KB
goal for the front page: 50 KB

Fix: make the front page plain HTML. Load the heavy parts only when someone signs in.

10 · When

Four steps. Each one ships on its own.

2 to 3 weeks
Fix the snags

Enter, Esc, links, blank conflict screen, tags that don't sync, the phone pop-up.

6 to 8 weeks
Table stakes

Spare key, note addresses, quick switcher, list view, smart search, light front page.

8 to 12 weeks
Depth

Pictures, importers, links between notes, locked tag names, the iPhone app.

Ongoing
Distinction

Simple tables, Face ID and passkeys, widgets, a public garden.

11 · Three old decisions to look at again

Keep the quiet. Drop three "nevers."

Was: no tables
Not databases. Just tables.

A packing list in three columns is Apple Notes territory, not Notion's.

Was: no backlinks
Not a graph. Just a link.

A note that can point to another note is what turns a pile into a body of work.

Was: no recovery codes
A spare key, on paper.

Zero-knowledge stays intact. Lost journals end.

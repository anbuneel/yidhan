# Archived brand artwork

Kept as history. None of it ships. The live logo is the leaf in `../source/`
(drawn by `scripts/brand/leaf.py`) and its flat exports in `../export/`
(`scripts/brand/flatten.py`, then `npm run icons:generate`).

- `enso/`: the hand-painted enso mark and lockup, the logo before 2026-11-09.
- `yi/`: the "yi" monogram icon, the sun-dot wordmark and their PNG exports,
  decided and replaced on 2026-11-09. Drawn by `scripts/brand/brand.py`.
- `sprout/`: the sprout and seal secondary artwork from the "yi" round.
